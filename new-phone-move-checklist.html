Honest limits — read these first

  • Carrier, SIM, and account-recovery problems belong to the carrier/provider. The checklist flags them early; it cannot fix them.
  • Guidance is dated November 2026 in the app. Makers can change their setup screens — the date is printed, never hidden.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: an actual two-phone transfer (the app moves no data, so there was nothing to test on hardware) and the storage-blocked session-only fallback (code-inspected).
