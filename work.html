Post a task — Hire a Hand

Your to-do list has things no app can do: check the roof after a storm, verify a business before you pay it, grab the Marketplace find before it's gone. Post the task on Human Hands, set your price, and a local Hand does it — you approve the photo proof before a cent releases. Posting free from Nov 10. From a quick photo check to the best tech support in the world — it's up to the buyer.

  • Business verification visit — before money moves to a business you've never seen. "About to wire money to a business you've never seen? Send a Hand first. Storefront, signage, and proof it's real — for the price of lunch, before your deposit is gone."
  • Marketplace pickup — the perfect find, forty minutes away, seller says first come. "Someone else is driving to buy it right now. Send a Hand first — eyes on it, photos to you, pickup handled before it's gone."

  • Vacant property check — an inherited or empty house, checked in person. "An empty house is a worry you can outsource. A local Hand checks it's locked, dry, and untouched — dated photos, every visit."

How posting works

  1. You post the task and set the price. The full posted price is the worker's pay — the worker keeps 100% of it, plus 100% of any tip.
  2. A local Hand claims it and does the work, then sends back the photo proof you asked for.
  3. You fund the job, then approve the proof. Posting is free. Once the operator approves your posting, you fund the job total — the posted price plus the 15% platform fee — through the job-payment link: buy.stripe.com — Human Hands Job Payment. Post a $40 task and the worker gets $40, the fee is $6, you fund $46. The operator holds the funding; the Hand does the work and sends photo proof; you approve the proof, and only then is the worker paid — 100% of the posted price, plus 100% of any tip.
  4. No prepaid wallet to fund. Some agent marketplaces make the poster pre-load a wallet balance before a task can even post. Human Hands has no wallet and no balance to keep topped up: posting is free, and you fund the one job — once, after the operator approves your posting — at the posted price plus the 15% fee.
  5. Paying is one tap from your phone. The person spending the money gets the easy end of it: pay with any major card, bank transfer, Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal — and at checkout, tap to pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Link — whichever your phone offers. Most jobs here are small, and a bunch of small jobs shouldn't mean pulling money out of a business account or writing a check every time.

An AI matches the best person for the job — matched in three tiers

Matching runs on skill, qualification tier, and distance — not on who you know, and not on how you got there.

Matching runs on three tiers, starting at the November 10 launch — every task is matched to the right Hand. Open Hands (18+, photo-proof checked) handle property checks, business verification, pickups, and visits. Skilled Hands bring their own tools for assembly, mounting, moving, and yard work — skills are self-declared and labeled as such. Licensed Pros are for electrical, plumbing, and HVAC work: they submit their state license, and we check it against the public registry before they can claim trade work — those jobs never go to anyone else. When you post, pick the tier the job needs (or let the category set it). If no qualified Hand is nearby yet, we'll say so — and offer to widen the search or hold your task on the waitlist.

From launch, Hands work in three tiers. Tier 1 — Open: any adult with a smartphone: checks, visits, pickups, pack & ship. Tier 2 — Skilled: you list your skills and bring your own tools — assembly, moving, yard work. Tier 3 — Licensed Pro: electricians, plumbers, HVAC techs. Send us your license number and a photo; we verify it against your state's public registry, and trade jobs are offered to you first — never to unlicensed workers. You'll only ever be offered tasks that fit your tier, your skills, and how you travel.

Post the task once, a matched local worker does the real-world part, photo proof comes back, and the worker is paid in full. You set the price; a flat 15% on top is the whole cost. One post, matched human, proof, done.
