What it does

  • Two-tap daily entry: mood on a plain 5-point scale (labels you can rename), activity tags in your own words with no cap, and an optional line of notes. One entry per day, editable any time.
  • Year grid: every day of the year colored by your recorded mood — your year at a glance, computed from your entries only.
  • Plain counts, honestly worded: counts and averages from your own entries only, stated as counts — for example, on the days you tagged something, your recorded mood averaged a number. A count from your log, never a cause, never a finding.
  • Full history, forever: no 30-day window on your own past.
  • Export always free: a full CSV of every entry plus a printable year review. Never gated.
  • Labeled SAMPLE month: a sample October to run the whole flow first, stored separately and removable without touching your real entries.

Safety and honest limits — read these first

  • A private record keeper only: it never diagnoses, never treats, and never gives mental-health advice. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services or the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (US) — not an app. That line is on the app's first screen too.
  • Counts are counts: the numbers describe what you recorded. They are not causes, not medical findings, and not insights into your health.
  • On this device only: no account, nothing uploaded, no sync. Export is the backup.
  • Free options exist, honestly: How We Feel is completely free with no paid tier (nonprofit, built with Yale's Center for Emotional Intelligence) — it is an emotion-vocabulary check-in app, not a user-tag day log with a printable year review.
