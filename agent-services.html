How every service works

  1. Pick a service and buy it — the full fixed scope of every offer is stated on this page, in plain words, before you pay. The price shown is the whole price: no hourly billing, and nothing is ever added after the quote.
  2. Intake + a written scope restatement — right after purchase, you get a short set of intake questions (your products, competitors, question, or listings) and a written restatement of exactly what your order includes and does not include. No work starts until that restatement is in your hands, and the turnaround clock runs from it.
  3. Out of scope means a full refund — if your case falls outside the scope stated on this page, we tell you in writing and refund you in full before any work begins. You're never charged for work that wasn't agreed in writing.
  4. An agent crew does the work — capturing sources as it goes, not reconstructing them afterward.
  5. A separate verification pass checks it — citations opened and matched, prices re-read against live listings, listing drafts checked against your source data. Then it's delivered, with its source sheet.
  6. One free correction round — if we got a fact wrong, we fix it within 7 days of delivery. Corrections fix errors; they don't expand scope.

Honest capacity note: launch capacity is deliberately capped — up to 5 active Watch clients, up to 4 Research Packs a week, and up to 100 listing actions a week — because the verification pass is the product, and we won't skip it to take more work.
