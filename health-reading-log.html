What it does

  • Two separate plain series: blood pressure (systolic, diastolic, and pulse) and weight (lb or kg, the unit you choose) — each entry date/time stamped, with a short note if you want one.
  • Add, edit, delete: your log, newest first, with All / Blood pressure / Weight views.
  • Averages, labeled honestly: the average of the readings you typed, computed as plain arithmetic and labeled exactly that — with the count of readings covered and the date range. Arithmetic, not a medical finding.
  • Printable clinician sheet: averages plus every reading on one print-clean page, with browser/device Print or Save-as-PDF instructions and a downloadable summary.
  • CSV export always free: every entry and every field, never gated.
  • Labeled SAMPLE readings: a full example set stored under its own key, removable without touching your real log.

Honest limits — read these first

  • This app does not measure anything: a web app cannot measure blood pressure. Readings are typed in from your own cuff or scale.
  • It never classifies, interprets, or judges a reading: no "high / normal / low" labels, no targets, no advice, ever. Averages are arithmetic on the readings you typed, not medical findings. If a reading concerns you, contact a clinician. This is a record keeper — not a medical device, not a monitoring or alert system.
  • Free options exist, honestly: Cuffio logs free on iPhone (its later visit PDFs are paid, $2.99/mo or $29.99 lifetime) and classifies readings; Cufflo's full history + CSV is £0.99/mo / £4.99/yr after a 7-day free history; SmartBP Premium is $39.99/yr and CalmBP Plus is $6.99/mo / $39.99/yr; your cuff maker's own app (OMRON, Withings, Apple Health) logs its own device free. This log is $3.99 once for the cross-platform owned file with blood pressure + weight together, uncapped history, and the clinician sheet.
  • Disclosed, not verified on your phone yet: the downloaded CSV and summary files' bytes could not be inspected in the audit sandbox; their columns and contents were verified in the app's source and the exports fired in the audit. Print rendering was verified as print-clean CSS with a downloadable summary fallback; the sandbox blocks the print dialog itself.
