What it does

  • Expectation card, not a guess: choose your work types (marketplaces, payment apps, freelance/gig work, rideshare/delivery, Human Hands, direct clients, a W-2 job) and your state — get the forms to watch for (1099-K, 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, W-2) and the thresholds that apply to you.
  • The season's real changes, sourced: the federal 1099-K line is over $20,000 AND over 200 transactions (IRS Fact Sheet 2025-08 / IR-2025-107; the $600 step-down is no longer in effect), and the 1099-NEC/MISC line rises from $600 to $2,000 for payments made after December 31, 2025. States that set their own lower 1099-K lines (MD, MA, VT, VA at $600; IL at $1,000 + 4 transactions) are shown; states not yet covered say so instead of guessing.
  • Gather-list + forms tracker: mileage, expenses, receipts, and payout exports on one local checklist — and check off each form as it arrives in January.
  • Deadline strip + preparer summary: the 2027 season's key dates in plain words, and a one-page summary to hand a tax preparer, generated from your entries on your phone.
  • Confusion log: the live contradictory claims ("the federal threshold is $600") each paired with the correcting IRS source.

Honest limits — read these first

  • Educational organizer only — this is not tax advice. It is not a tax preparer and not a filing service. It never tells you what you owe and never decides what you must file; it shows what to expect and watch for.
  • A missing form does not erase income. The app says this plainly, because it is true whether a platform sends you a form or not.
  • Every rule is dated ("Checked Nov 2026") with its source named. Rules change — confirm with the IRS or your state source before filing.
  • No account, no cloud: what you type never leaves your phone.
