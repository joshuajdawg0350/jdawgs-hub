Who this is for

You sell on eBay, Mercari, or Facebook Marketplace and you have a day job: the item sold, the buyer is waiting, and the post office closes before you get home. A local Hand packs the item properly, applies your label, drops it at the carrier, and sends you the tracking-receipt photo the same day — your buyer sees movement, your ratings stay clean, and nobody has to leave work early.

How it works

  1. Post the job and set your price. Say what the item is, where it is, and where it's dropped off. The full posted price is the worker's pay.
  2. A local Hand claims it and ships it today, photographing the packed item and the drop-off receipt.
  3. You approve the photo proof, then payment releases. You pay your posted price plus a 15% platform fee — post a $40 job and the worker gets $40, the fee is $6, you pay $46. Nothing is due before the proof is in and approved.
