What it is

This is a music-making studio you open in a web browser. No download, no install, no account, no subscription. All you need is your phone — that's all you need.

You build beats on a drum machine. You write melodies on real synthesizers. You put your voice onto your beats. You chop and loop audio, stack harmonies, mix it, master it loud enough for streaming, and arrange it all into a full song. Any style. Any genre. And when it's done, it's ready to publish — the finished file carries your name and your song's title inside it.

When you buy it, you also get it as a single HTML file you can keep and run yourself. One payment of $19.99. Yours forever.

What it does

  • Drum machine & step sequencer — lay down kicks, snares, hats, claps, 808s, and more, step by step
  • Synthesizers + piano roll — multiple synth sounds; draw melodies and basslines note by note
  • Your voice on your beats — record vocals or instruments straight from your phone's browser, right over the track
  • Chord tools — build chord progressions without music-theory homework
  • Sample Lab — record a sound or bring one in, chop it up, and play it like an instrument
  • The Edit Room — bring in any audio file, then cut, trim, loop it (with smooth crossfades so loops don't click), and rearrange it until it's right
  • Drop builder — the big moment in every track: duck the beat, add a riser, land an isolated sub hit
  • Harmony stacking — add 3rd and 5th harmony layers to a melody or vocal line automatically
  • Song Builder — line up your beats and parts and build a whole song, start to finish
  • Live Band mode — play it live, like a band on a stage, not just a grid on a screen
  • Acoustic, full depth — a whole acoustic side of the studio: guitar, piano, and more, built deep, not tacked on
  • 21 genre packs + a feel engine — load a style and go; the feel engine makes the rhythm move like a person played it, not a machine
  • Quick Split — pull a recording apart into its parts, to remix it or fix it. Plain talk: it is not AI stem separation, and the app says so right on it
  • Effects rack — EQ, reverb, delay/echo, distortion, compression, chorus
  • Full mixer — per-track volume and pan, mute and solo
  • Loudness mastering — normalize to streaming loudness with a limiter, so your track doesn't sound quiet next to everyone else's
  • Release-ready export — MP3 + WAV with your song's title and your name embedded in the file, ready to publish
  • Cover maker — make the cover art for your song without leaving the studio
  • Publish guide — plain steps for putting your finished song out where people can hear it
  • Runs anywhere — in the browser on your phone or computer, and as a single downloadable HTML file that's yours to keep

Easy to use as possible for a human: if you can tap a grid, you can make a beat in the first five minutes.
