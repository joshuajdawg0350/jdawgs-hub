What it does

  • Every vehicle, one log: service entries (date, odometer, job, cost, shop or DIY, notes) and fuel entries for multiple vehicles, all stored on your device.
  • Honest MPG: MPG is computed only between full fills, with the method printed in plain words — a partial fill shows "needs another full fill," never a made-up number. Cost per mile comes from your own entries, labeled as arithmetic.
  • Due next, by miles or months: set rules like oil every 5,000 miles or 6 months; the app shows Overdue / Due soon / OK with the last-done and next-due numbers, and exports .ics calendar reminders (7-day and 1-day alarms).
  • Export always free: full-history CSV plus a printable report, available from the first entry — never gated, never paywalled. That is the whole point.
  • Try it first: a labeled SAMPLE vehicle runs log → due-next → export end to end, and removes in one tap.

Honest limits — read these first

  • This app cannot read your car's computer. It does not read diagnostic codes and does not diagnose check-engine lights — no web app honestly can without a separate scanner device.
  • The math is only as good as the numbers typed in. Wrong odometer or gallons in, wrong MPG out — the app shows its method so you can check it.
  • Reminders are calendar files you import (.ics), not push notifications from us.
  • Records live on this device/browser only. The free CSV/report export is your backup — use it.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: the downloaded CSV/.ics file bytes on a real phone (contents verified via the printable report, record counter, and code inspection), the receipt-photo file flow on a real device, and the storage-blocked session-only path (code-inspected; the audit browser allows storage).
