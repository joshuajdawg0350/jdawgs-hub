What it does

  • Seven synthesized layers, or start with the SAMPLE: Rain, Ocean surf, Fan, Night, and White / Pink / Brown noise — every one generated in code on your phone with the Web Audio API. Tap "Try the starter mix — SAMPLE" and hear the synthesis quality in one tap.
  • Mix it your way: each layer has its own volume slider and mute, plus a master volume, a big Play/Pause, and a live waveform that moves only when sound is actually playing.
  • Recipes: save a mix under your own name, recall it in one tap, rename or delete it anytime. Three starter recipes are built in and labeled as starting points. Recipes save on your device only.
  • Sleep timer with a real fade: 15 / 30 / 60 / 90 minutes or your own number — the final stretch fades smoothly to silence on real gain automation, then playback stops. "Play all night" is an explicit choice, and Cancel restores full volume.
  • Built for the dark: near-black design, big low-light controls, no bright flashes, no ads — ever. An ad at 3 a.m. is this category's worst sin, and this file has none.

Honest limits — read these first

  • Synthesized, not recorded: the rain and ocean are generated by code. They are continuous with no loop seams, and they are not field recordings of real places. If you want real recordings, myNoise has a free tier — it is named in the app too, along with Veil, another synthesized mixer.
  • No health claims: this app plays sounds you mix. It does not treat insomnia or any condition, does not track or measure your sleep, and makes no medical promise.
  • Browser playback limits: background and lock-screen playback depend on your phone OS keeping the browser alive; installing to the home screen is the recommended setup. A silenced phone or a killed browser tab stops the sound — no browser app can promise otherwise.
  • Disclosed, not verified on your phone yet: in the audit sandbox, page time is frozen, so the timer's final fade-to-zero could not be watched elapsing — the fade is real gain automation in the code, the countdown and Cancel were verified live, and the full short-timer fade plus audible quality on your phone's speaker are the remaining owner checks.
