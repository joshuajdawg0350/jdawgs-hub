What it does

  • Batch in, or start with the built-in SAMPLE: pick a whole item's photo set from your device at once. A labeled SAMPLE set (a pocket knife in three shots, with a deliberate scratch kept visible) runs the whole flow first.
  • Every marketplace's size in one pass: pick eBay, Etsy, Amazon, Meta, and Trading Post — each shown with its published size, ratio, and main-photo rules (checked Oct 18, 2026) — and the set exports at every chosen platform's spec, with a crop-position slider per photo and platform-named files. Exports never upscale a small photo into a fake big one.
  • Honest light fix: brightness, contrast, and exposure correction on a real histogram, with measured before/after mean luminance and clipping counts from your actual pixels, and a one-tap revert. Labeled an image adjustment — never "AI enhancement."
  • Spec checklist per photo: too small for the platform, wrong ratio, the eBay rule against added text or watermarks on main photos (a rule reminder), and a framing guide for Amazon's 85% fill rule that you judge yourself.
  • Condition-honesty exports: side-by-side before/after pairs and a multi-angle collage on a plain labeled mat — flaws stay in frame by design, so a buyer sees the real item.

Honest limits — read these first

  • No background removal and no AI anything: a browser canvas cannot honestly cut a subject out of a cluttered photo. Tools that promise it are cloud AI with subscriptions and credits. This studio does sizing, framing, light, checklists, pairs, and collages. If you need a real AI cutout, Photoroom is that tool — its paid plans start at $12.99/mo and its free tier is personal use only.
  • Correction cannot create detail: upscaling a 400 px photo to 1,600 px makes a bigger file, not a sharper photo — the checklist flags under-size originals instead of faking them.
  • Marketplace rules change: specs are shown with their check date (Oct 18, 2026) and are guidance, not a guarantee a listing will be accepted. Confirm before a big upload.
  • Nothing leaves your device: no account, no cloud, no sync, no export caps, no credits. Very large batches are bounded by phone memory; the app states a practical per-batch guide.
  • Disclosed, not verified on your phone yet: the real-photo file-picker path runs the same pipeline as the fully verified SAMPLE path but was not exercised with a user file in the audit browser, and downloaded files were confirmed via the page's own completion status rather than inspected on a real device — a real-phone check with your own photos is the remaining owner check.
