What it does

  • Unlimited groups and expenses: multiple named groups, no daily cap, no cooldown, no counter anywhere.
  • Three split methods with printed arithmetic: equal, exact amounts, or percentages — each person's share is shown as the division that produced it. A percentage set that does not add to 100 is refused in plain words, never quietly stretched. Cent rounding: the largest share absorbs the remainder, and the rule is stated in-app.
  • Balances + settle-up plan: per-person balances per currency, and a fewest-transfers plan whose steps are narrated in plain words so anyone can check it.
  • Multi-currency honesty: currencies stay separate until you type a conversion rate; converted figures are labeled with that rate and its date.
  • Export always free: a full CSV (one row per share, every field) plus a printable group summary. Never gated.
  • Labeled SAMPLE trip: a 3-person weekend with 12 expenses, including one 5-expense day, to run the whole flow first.

Honest limits — read these first

  • Single-device, one keeper: it does not sync between phones and nobody else can log into it. If your group wants everyone entering expenses live, Tricount (free) and Splitwise do that job — this app does not pretend to.
  • It does not move money: no payments are sent or collected; settle-up is a plan, paid however the group pays.
  • On this device only: no account, nothing uploaded. A cleared browser can lose a ledger that was never exported — export is the backup.
  • Free options exist, honestly: Tricount is 100% free and unlimited for group IOUs (export and statistics removed); Splid is free for one group with a $3.99–$4.99 one-time unlock for unlimited groups and export. This ledger is $3.99 once for unlimited groups + printed arithmetic + always-free CSV/printable export in an owned offline file.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: the CSV download completed with its confirmation, but the downloaded file's bytes could not be captured inside the audit sandbox — the generator was verified in code (one row per share, every field). The storage-blocked fallback is implemented but was not triggered live (the audit browser allows storage).
