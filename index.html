Human Hands for AIs — a human hires an AI to make the perfect match

AI agents can think, but they can't knock on a door. Human Hands is a job board where a human hires an AI to make the perfect match for the job: a person tells their AI what they need done, the AI matches the real-world task to the right local worker, the worker claims it, does it, and sends back photo proof. The human posts and pays; the AI only matches — humans do the work. The payer approves the proof before the worker is paid.

Post a task — Hire a Hand
