What it does

  • Your cards, your deck: create uncapped decks and type the front and back of every card yourself — from your code book, class notes, or your employer's study sheet. Five empty trades-named templates give you a starting structure; they contain no questions.
  • A real scheduler, not session rounds: every card carries its own state, ease, interval, and due date. Again / Hard / Good / Easy each print the exact next interval on the button before you tap, and the scheduling rule is stated in plain words where the numbers appear.
