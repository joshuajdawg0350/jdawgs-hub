What it does

  • Generic stash, any craft: supplies typed by you — name, quantity + unit, color/brand, where it lives (bin, shelf, room), optional photo kept on your device only.
  • Projects link to the stash: each project lists the items and quantities it needs, typed from your own pattern. The app shows, per line, “You have enough” or “Still need X” — plain needs-minus-on-hand arithmetic.
  • Shopping list of the shortfall only: for one project or all projects — copyable, downloadable, and print-clean. You buy only what you are actually missing.
  • Uncapped everything: no 3-, 5-, or 10-item wall anywhere. CSV export of stash and projects is always free.
  • Labeled SAMPLE stash + project: run the whole flow first; samples remove cleanly without touching your real entries.

Honest limits — read these first

  • A stash record, not a pattern library and not a label scanner — it never reads a yarn label from a photo, never identifies a fiber, and never computes the yardage a pattern needs. You type what your pattern says; the app does the subtraction.
  • Quantities are your counts — the shortfall is arithmetic on the numbers you entered, nothing more.
  • Free and craft-specific options exist, honestly: the incumbents above are strong single-craft apps (YarnBuddy for knit/crochet, Spindrift for spinning, Flossom for cross stitch) with free tiers up to their caps. This log is $3.99 once for a different thing: one generic, uncapped log for every craft in an owned offline file, with export never gated.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: the CSV download buttons were present and wired, but their downloaded file bytes were not separately captured in the audit session; printing works through your browser’s own Print (the in-app print button was removed because the audit sandbox blocks it). Records live on this device only; export a CSV as your backup.
