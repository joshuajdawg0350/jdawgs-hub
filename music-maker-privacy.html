The short version

J-Dawgs Universal Music Maker collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server. The app holds no permission to reach the internet at all, and it works fully offline. Its one sensitive permission is the microphone, and that is used only when you tap Record, to record your own voice or instruments.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app makes stays on your device:

  • While you work: your project — beats, recordings, edits — is held inside the app itself, only while the app is open. The app uses no browser storage of any kind (no local storage, no cookies, no on-device database), so if you close the app without exporting, unsaved work is lost. The app tells you this plainly: export to keep your work forever.
  • When you export: a finished song (MP3 or WAV), voice takes, stems, MIDI, a project file, or cover art is written only where you choose to save it on your device. From there it is your file. It leaves your device only if you share it yourself, using your phone's own tools, outside this app.
  • Files you bring in: the app can open an audio, image, or project file only when you pick it yourself in the file picker — to import a sound, open a project, or set cover art. It never browses your files on its own.
  • One external link: the app contains a link to our support page. It opens in your browser only if you tap it; what your browser does after that is governed by your browser, not by this app.

The microphone

The app's only Android permission is the microphone (RECORD_AUDIO). It is used for exactly one thing: recording your voice or an instrument when you tap Record. Recordings are processed and kept on your device only. They are never sent to us or to anyone else — the app has no INTERNET permission and contains no code that transmits data anywhere.

What we collect

  • Personal data: none
  • Your recordings, songs, and project files: never collected — they are created, processed, and kept on your device only
  • Usage / analytics data: none
  • Device identifiers: none
  • Location: none
  • Contacts, camera: never accessed

Sharing

We share nothing with anyone, because we receive nothing. We sell no data — there is no data to sell.

Deleting your data

Everything the app holds lives on your device, and only inside the app while it is open. Closing the app clears any work you have not exported, and uninstalling the app removes the app and anything it was holding. Files you exported are your own files on your device — delete them the same way you would delete any other file. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.

Children

J-Dawgs Universal Music Maker is a music-creation tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version of this app ever changes what it does with data, this policy will be updated and dated before that version ships.
