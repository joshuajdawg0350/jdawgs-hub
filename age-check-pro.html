What it does

Pick 21+ or 18+ mode first — before any scan — then check the physical card three ways: camera barcode scan where your browser provides a built-in reader, a scanner-gun or paste-in of the barcode data, or manual date-of-birth entry for worn cards and bad light. You get a full-screen PASS / FAIL verdict with the customer's age in years, a separate ID EXPIRED flag, and a CAUTION — inspect the physical card verdict when barcode data is malformed or inconsistent.

Every check lands in a timestamped compliance log — the headline feature. The log stores no customer name, no date of birth, and no license number: only the time, the mode, the result, the issuing state, a staff/device label, and whether the check was a scan or manual. Search it, export it as CSV, and set it to auto-purge (90 days by default). A local house list covers banned or regular customers. A clearly labeled SAMPLE mode lets you run the whole flow end-to-end with synthetic data that never mixes into your real log.

How it works

1. Set 21+ or 18+ mode.   2. Scan, paste, or type the date of birth.   3. Read the verdict across the counter.   4. The check is logged — export the CSV whenever you need the record.

Honest limits — read these before you buy

  • It does not verify mobile IDs. Version 1 checks physical cards only. Mobile-ID acceptance is covered by the Kit's Readiness Guide using the free official verifier apps.
  • It does not authenticate IDs. It reads the barcode, checks format consistency, expiry, and the age math. A high-quality fake carrying someone else's real data in a well-formed barcode will pass — the same limit as every barcode-only tool. It never claims to catch fakes.
  • Camera scanning depends on your browser. Decoding uses your browser's built-in barcode reader where it provides one; where it doesn't, the paste/scanner and manual-entry paths do the same job and the age math is identical.
  • Not an official or state tool, and not legal advice. Whether a logged check satisfies your state's rules is your counsel's call.
  • Real-card scan rates vary with lighting, card wear, and the phone camera — that is exactly why manual entry is a first-class path, not a footnote.
