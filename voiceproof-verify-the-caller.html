Why this works

Every voice-clone scam runs the same play: a familiar voice, a crisis, a deadline, and secrecy. The protocol breaks all four. You slow it down (crisis and deadline die), you call back on a number you hold (the spoofed or cloned channel dies), and you ask for the code (the missing secret dies). A legitimate caller — a real son, a real boss, a real bank — will pass every step without complaint. Only a scammer needs you to skip them.

If a call fails: hang up, send nothing, and log it in the Scam Call Log — then report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. If money already moved, call your bank on the number on your card, immediately.
