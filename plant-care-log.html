Plant apps charge $30–$48 a year for a watering schedule — and cap the free tier at 5–15 plants, right where a real collection starts. This log is the schedule keeper only: your plants, the intervals you set, a due-next list that rolls forward when you tap done, and a care history in one file you own. $4.99 once.

What it does

  • Per-plant cards: name, room, an optional photo kept on your device, and watering (+ optional fertilizing) intervals you set yourself.
  • Due-next home: every plant sorted by its real next due date, with plain overdue wording.
  • Done means done: tap Watered or Fed and the next due date rolls forward from today — and the tap writes the care-history entry at the same time.
  • Calendar reminders: one .ics file with an event per due item, alarms 7 days before and on the day.
  • Exports always free: CSV export and a printable list, generated locally. No plant cap anywhere.
  • Labeled SAMPLE shelf: sample plants run the whole flow and remove cleanly without touching your real plants or history.

Honest limits — read these first

  • A schedule keeper only — it never identifies a plant, diagnoses disease or pests, or computes how much water a species needs. Intervals are yours (or your plant tag’s).
  • Reminders are calendar files, not a notification service. Records are what you type; nothing is uploaded; it works offline.
  • Free/other options exist, honestly: GreenThumb is free with ads (identification-led), and one-time rivals Repot and Greenroot sit at $14.99 — this log is $4.99, ad-free, and yours as a file.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: an actual photo file pick on a real phone (the on-device FileReader path is code-verified; the audit harness could not drive a file pick), and printing from a regular browser (the printable sheet and print CSS are built and verified in-page).
