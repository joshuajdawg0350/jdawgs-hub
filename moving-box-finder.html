What it does

  • Boxes first: each box gets a number/name, a destination room, and a current location (Truck / Garage / Storage / In the house, plus your own).
  • Contents, typed by you: items per box with optional quantity and a fragile flag, plus an optional photo kept on your device.
  • Find it: one search over item names shows the box, its destination room, and where it is now.
  • Move-day views: boxes grouped by destination room or by current location.
  • Unpacked check-off: mark a box unpacked and it stays in your history — nothing is deleted.
  • Exports always free: a printable box list and a CSV export, generated locally.
  • Labeled SAMPLE move: 4 boxes and 12 items run the whole flow and remove cleanly without touching your real move.

Honest limits — read these first

  • Manual entry only — no barcode scanning, no AI identification of contents, ever claimed.
  • The app finds the box you recorded; if the record is wrong, the answer is wrong. Photos stay on the device; nothing is uploaded; records are what you type.
  • Free/other options exist, honestly: a no-item-limit insurance inventory exists at $11.99/yr (Manifest) — it is a subscription inventory, not a box-first moving catalog in a file you own.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: the CSV download landing and the photo picker flow on a real phone (both code-verified; the audit session could not test file downloads).
