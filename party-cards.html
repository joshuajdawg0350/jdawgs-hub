What it does

  • Every deck included: Around the House, Worksite & Tools, Animals & Nature, At the Movies, Holidays & Seasons, For Kids, Sports & Games, Food & Drink — 32 cards each, with the real count printed on the deck picker. No deck is ever sold separately.
  • Real rounds, big taps: pick a deck, 3-2-1, then 30, 60, or 90 seconds with one word in huge type. CORRECT and PASS are big 88px tap targets — no tilt controls to misfire mid-round. Cards shuffle every round and never repeat inside one.
  • One phone, whole group: end-of-round lists show exactly what was got and passed, and the handoff screen — "Pass the phone — tap when ready" — passes the phone cleanly to the next player.
  • Two-team mode: teams alternate rounds and the totals are shown as plain arithmetic with every round listed. No hidden scoring rules.
  • Make your own deck: type your words, name the deck, it joins the picker with its real count. Decks live on your device only and export as plain text whenever you want — always free.
  • Try it first: a labeled 12-card SAMPLE deck runs a full round before you commit to a deck.

Honest limits — read these first

  • Taps, not tilt: this app does not use motion sensors — a web app's motion access is permission-gated and unreliable, so honest big taps are the control, stated up front.
  • One phone, one room: there is no online multiplayer, no remote play, and no score sharing. Your scores live on this phone. That is the whole design — a group in a room, one phone.
  • The words are ours: every card was written for this game; charades itself is a classic party format and free versions exist. What you pay for is every deck included, custom decks included, no ads, and a file you own.
  • Disclosed, not verified: the plain-text deck export was verified on screen (text, Download and Copy controls); the downloaded file landing on a specific phone could not be observed in the audit sandbox.
