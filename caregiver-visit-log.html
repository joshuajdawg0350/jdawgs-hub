What it does

  • Per-visit entries: date, who visited (a plain name you type — no accounts for anyone), what was done from a checklist you can edit, and a note.
  • Last visit / next planned home: computed from your recorded entries, so anyone holding the phone can see when the last visit was and when the next one is planned.
  • Recurring care tasks: tasks with due-next dates; tapping Done rolls the next due date forward from the day you did it — the rule is stated in the app.
  • Printable handover sheet: recent visits plus what the next visitor needs to know, on one print-clean page — the sharing mechanism, stated plainly.
  • CSV export always free: every visit and task field, never gated.
  • Labeled SAMPLE family: a full example family stored under its own key, removable without touching your real record.

Honest limits — read these first

  • A record keeper only: it never gives medical or care advice, never judges health, and is not a monitoring or alert system. If the person seems unwell, contact a clinician or emergency services — do not consult a log.
  • Single device, no sync: no account, no cloud. Sharing is the printed handover sheet, never live sync.
  • Shared platforms exist, honestly: Caring Village (free for 1 village / 2 members, then $14.99–$24.99/mo), CarePack (owner $5.99/mo / $49.99/yr), Private Care Pro ($4.99–$49.99/mo, built for paid caregivers), and Neela ($12/mo) are genuinely better at live multi-person coordination. This log is $3.99 once for the one-keeper case: a private record in a file you own.
