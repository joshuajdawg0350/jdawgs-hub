What it does

  • Open a clip, or start with the built-in SAMPLE: MP4/WebM/MOV from your device (short-form lane, about 3 minutes), on a 9:16 stage with a fit-or-crop reframe choice. A labeled SAMPLE clip is generated in the app so you can run the whole flow first.
  • Hook first: a 0–3 second hook card built from your own typed words, with three blank fill-in prompt shapes — or the hook rendered as your opening caption instead.
  • Captions from your own words: paste or type your transcript, the app splits it into short chunks, and you time them by tapping as you hear them land (or auto-spread plus nudge buttons). Three original caption styles, every caption editable and deletable.
  • Safe zones: pick TikTok, Reels, or Shorts and that platform's UI danger zones are drawn over your actual frame — captions and the hook warn before they land under a username, a progress bar, or the action rail.
  • Speech loudness fix: optional compression + limiting to a measured −1 dBFS ceiling, with a measured before/after peak and RMS table and A/B preview — numbers from your clip, never a "% louder" claim.
  • Exports: burned-in video recorded in real time and labeled with the format your browser truly recorded, an instant SRT caption file, and a hook-card PNG.

Honest limits — read these first

  • No AI auto-captions: captions are your own words, timed by you. Browsers' speech recognition is inconsistent and often cloud-backed — it would break the no-upload promise, so it is excluded on purpose. Your words are right because they are yours.
  • Export is real-time and device-dependent: a 60-second clip takes about 60 seconds to export; quality follows your device and browser encoder, and the file is labeled with the format the browser actually recorded — never a relabeled file. If your browser offers no usable video format, the video button says so plainly and the SRT + hook-card PNG still export.
  • It cannot post for you, use another app's music library, or beat a native editor on 4K/long-form work. For Reels-only free editing, Instagram's own Edits app is free and is named here honestly. This studio's ground is: no account, no upload, platform-neutral safe zones, the hook step, the loudness fix, and an owned offline file.
  • Nothing leaves your device: no account, no cloud project, no sync. Export the SRT to keep your timings — closing without it loses them, and the app says so early, not just at the end.
  • Disclosed, not verified on your phone yet: the hook card's on-canvas pixels during playback and the crop/karaoke/boxed style variants run through the same verified render path as the screenshot- and PNG-verified captions but were not individually screenshot-checked in the audit; a real phone's file picker with a real camera clip is the remaining owner check.
