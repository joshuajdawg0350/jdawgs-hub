Every pet household keeps the same scattered record — vaccines in a vet PDF, meds on a sticky note, weight in memory. This log keeps it all in one owned file: unlimited pets, due dates sorted with overdue flagged, and a vet-ready report that is always free. $4.99 once.

What it does

  • Unlimited pets: a full record per pet — name, species/breed, birthday/age note — with no pet cap, ever.
  • Weight log: dated entries with the change since the last entry computed from your own numbers.
  • Vaccines & medications: each with the due-next date you (or your vet's paperwork) supplied, sorted soonest first, overdue flagged in plain words with day counts.
  • Vet visits: date, reason, cost, and notes — the history a new vet actually asks for.
  • Reminders that are honest: a .ics calendar file per due date with alarms 7 days before and 1 day before. No fake push notifications.
  • Vet-ready report — always free: one clear printable page per pet (profile, weight history, vaccines, meds, visits) plus a full CSV export — never gated.
  • Try it first: a labeled SAMPLE pet runs the whole flow and removes cleanly without touching your real pets.

Honest limits — read these first

  • A record keeper, not veterinary advice. It never diagnoses, doses, or judges whether a vaccine or medication is needed.
  • Due dates are yours. They are the dates you or your vet's paperwork supplied — the app does not compute medical schedules.
  • Reminders are calendar files (.ics), not a notification service.
  • Records are only what you type. Nothing is uploaded; there is no account; everything stays on your device.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: the Print button is blocked inside the sandboxed preview — the printable report and browser-menu Print instructions work when the file is opened in a normal browser; real-phone .ics/CSV downloads share the standard disclosed class for our apps.
