What it does

  • Your photo, your words: load a photo (file only) or use a built-in SAMPLE, add 1–3 lines of title text with original big-text treatments and high-contrast colour pairs. No AI generation.
  • Legibility checker: one tap renders your design at 360px, 168px, and 150px wide — the sizes feeds actually show — so you see exactly what survives.
  • Safe zones on your artwork: centre safe area (1100×620), 5% edge padding, bottom-right timestamp shield, and a vertical 9:16 guide set. Warnings are measured from your title's real position — warn, never block.
  • A/B pair view: save Variant A, adjust, save Variant B, compare both at feed size. You decide — the app makes no CTR prediction and gives no score.
  • Measured exports: JPG/PNG at exact 1280×720 (YouTube) and 1080×1920 (vertical), platform-named files, real byte size shown before download, with a warning if a file crosses the 2 MB phone-upload limit.
  • Try it first: a labelled SAMPLE runs the full flow — treatment, legibility strip, safe zones, A/B, export.

Honest limits — read these first

  • No AI, no CTR score: the app does not create images and does not predict clicks — no honest offline tool can. It proves legibility and placement with measurements.
  • Canva free exists: Canva's free tier makes correctly sized thumbnails with no watermark and is named in the app as the free alternative. Our difference is no account, no upload, and the proof workflow in a file you own.
  • It cannot post or live-test: a browser app cannot post to YouTube or run a live A/B test on real viewers — YouTube's own Test & Compare does that for eligible channels, after publishing. Exports are files you upload yourself.
  • Disclosed, not verified: native file-picker upload of a user photo and the final download handoff could not be driven in the audit sandbox; both use the same standard canvas paths exercised by the verified SAMPLE/export flow.
