Voiceproofing™

A new category, from J-Dawgs Industries. Voiceproofing is how a family or a small business proves that a voice on the phone is really who it claims to be — using shared secrets and rotating spoken codes instead of trusting a voice, now that any voice can be cloned from seconds of audio.

Why this category exists now

For a hundred years, recognizing a voice was the verification. That ended when cloning got cheap. What replaced it, for households, was… nothing. Banks got attestation systems, carriers verified phone numbers (not people), and families got advice articles. The tools were never built. So we built them — all of them, one shelf, free.

The kit — the whole shelf, free

📟 Voiceproof CallCode™ — flagship

Rotating spoken verification codes from a shared secret. Both phones compute the same 6 digits + word for the same 5 minutes; optionally bound to a dollar amount. A cloned voice can't produce it.

🔑 Voiceproof CodeWord Forge™

Forge your circle's code word, a separate duress word that silently means "this isn't real," and backup questions whose answers aren't on social media.

📞 Voiceproof Verify-the-Caller™

The mid-call protocol runner for five situations — with the exact words to say — plus a red-flag checklist with a live risk readout.

🧲 Voiceproof Family Card™

A printable fridge & wallet card: real callback numbers, code word, duress word, and the four-step rule. Nothing to remember in a crisis.

🎯 Voiceproof Scam Drill™

Eight realistic scam scripts — grandparent emergency, boss's voice note, change order, bank fraud dept — with decision points, scoring, and the tell explained.

🏢 Voiceproof Callback Policy Builder™

For businesses: a printable one-page Voice Payment Authorization Policy — no payment on a voice instruction alone, callback thresholds, sign-off line.

📓 Voiceproof Scam Call Log™

Log suspicious calls in the fields ReportFraud.ftc.gov and the FBI IC3 ask for. On-device only; exports CSV and a printable report.

⏱️ Set up in ~10 minutes

1) Forge a code word. 2) Print the Family Card. 3) Save everyone's real numbers. 4) Run one drill together. That's a voiceproofed circle — before the scary call ever comes.
