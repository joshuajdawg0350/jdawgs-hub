J-Dawgs Before-the-Storm Property Documentation Kit™

After storm damage, a claim lives or dies on the "before" photos most people never took. This kit walks you through them in calm weather — room by room, plus the exterior from the ground — telling you what to shoot and why an adjuster wants it, then stamps every photo into a dated, hash-listed evidence report you keep. It runs on your phone, entirely offline. Your photos never leave your device.

What it does

  • A guided walk, not a blank folder: 11 spaces — entry/living, kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms, closets and cabinets, laundry, garage, attic access (only where it's safe), the exterior and roofline, yard/fences/outbuildings, and the water heater, electrical panel, and shutoff valves. Every prompt says what to photograph and why it matters, including serial and model plates on major appliances and electronics.
  • Three season modes: Winter Storm (pipes, roof load, trees, heating, gutters and drainage — the mode for right now), Hurricane, and Wildfire. Same walk; the exterior and utility prompts change with the season.
  • Evidence list with real hashes: every photo gets a SHA-256 hash computed on your device, plus a hash for the whole set, in a dated evidence list. The report states exactly what a hash proves — the file hasn't changed since it was hashed — and what it doesn't.
  • One report you keep: a printable evidence report generated on your phone, with timestamp-strip photo copies (your originals are never modified), your checklist completion, and the full hash list — ready to store or send to your insurer.
  • Try the whole thing in 30 seconds: a built-in SAMPLE property, clearly labeled, is pre-loaded so you can run the full walk and the export before photographing anything real.
  • Private by design: no cloud, no account, no upload. Photos stay on the device; progress saves on the device.

Honest limits — read these first

  • Documentation helps a claim; it does not guarantee coverage or a payout. This app is not insurance advice, and no app can promise what a policy will pay.
  • What the hash proves — and only that: a SHA-256 hash proves a file is unchanged since the moment it was hashed on your device. It does not prove where or when a photo was truly taken beyond your device's clock. We will never call this "court-proof" or "insurer-certified" — nobody honest can.
  • Ground only: every roof prompt is photograph-from-the-ground. The app never asks you to climb a roof or a ladder for a photo, and the safety line is inside the walk itself.
  • Timeliness, stated precisely: NOAA's winter outlook issued October 15, 2026 favors wetter-than-average conditions across the South and East for December 2026–February 2027, with a strong El Niño — winter documentation is timely. No 2027 hurricane-season outlook has been published, and we don't claim one.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: the phone's own camera/file-picker step couldn't be driven by our test rig (your photos use the same intake and hashing path the audit exercised with the sample set), and the dark color scheme is built from our design tokens but was screenshot-checked in the light scheme.
