What it does

  • Invoice in under a minute: your business block (name, phone, and payment instructions in your own words — e.g., the Venmo or Cash App handle you type), the customer, and line items for labor and materials. Totals are computed live and shown before you export.
  • Estimates that convert: the same form in Estimate mode, with a validity line; one tap converts an estimate to an invoice keeping every line.
  • A real PDF, made on your phone: generated locally, multi-page when your lines overflow, no watermark, and no branding on it but your own.
  • A ledger that never locks you out: every invoice and estimate saved on your device with Paid / Unpaid / Sent status and a still-owed total (arithmetic on your entries, labeled as such). The full ledger exports as CSV any time — always available, never gated.
  • Try it first: a labeled SAMPLE invoice runs the whole flow — form, totals, PDF — end to end.

Honest limits — read these first

  • This app makes the document and keeps your local list. It does not send invoices for you, collect or process payments, or connect to any bank or payment service. The payment instructions on your invoice are words you typed.
  • It is not accounting or tax software: no bookkeeping, no tax filing, no tax advice. The tax % field applies the rate you enter — the app does not know your rate.
  • Records live on this device and browser only. Clearing browser data can erase them — the CSV and PDF exports are your backup, and the app says so.
  • The free lanes exist and we name them: Zoho Invoice is free but capped at 500 invoices a year with Zoho branding on your documents; Wave's free plan is US/Canada only and sends through Wave. This app's difference is no account, no caps, no one else's branding, and it works offline on a job site.
