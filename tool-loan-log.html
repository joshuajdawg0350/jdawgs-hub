J-Dawgs Tool & Equipment Loan Log™

Tools walk off job sites and out of garages — lent to a coworker, a neighbor, another crew — and nobody wrote it down. This log writes it down in seconds: who has it, when it is due back, and one tap when it comes home. $3.99 once.

What it does

  • Lend entry in seconds: tool, who has it, due-back date (lent date and a +7-day due date are prefilled) — three taps plus typing, with an optional photo of the tool going out.
  • Out-now list: everything currently lent, sorted by due date, with overdue items flagged in plain words ("Overdue by 2 days"), computed from the real dates.
  • One-tap returned: mark it home, add an optional return photo and condition note, and the loan moves to history with its lent, due, and returned dates intact.
  • Reminders that are honest: a .ics calendar file per loan with alarms before the due date. No fake push notifications.
  • History export, always free: a printable report plus a CSV of every loan — never gated, never paywalled.
  • Try it first: labeled SAMPLE loans (drill, ladder, saw) run the full lend-to-return flow and remove cleanly without touching your real entries.
  • No borrower account, ever: the person borrowing your tool never touches this app, creates nothing, and gets nothing installed.

Honest limits — read these first

  • This app records what you enter. It cannot track a tool's location — there is no GPS, Bluetooth, or tag tracking — and it cannot message the borrower.
  • A log entry is documentation, not proof in a dispute. It is your written record, stated plainly — never sold as evidence or protection.
  • Records live on this device only. The history export is your backup — use it.
  • Reminders are calendar files (.ics), not a notification service.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: the photo pickers on a real phone (standard file/camera inputs) and downloaded files landing in a real Downloads folder.
