What it is

Hustle City is a street-trading game in the classic buy-low/sell-high mold, built as a single HTML file that plays in the browser on your phone — no download from an app store, no install, no account. You start with $2,000 in your pocket and a $5,500 debt to the loan shark, and that debt grows 10% every day. You have 30 in-game days to work the city, build a fortune, and pay the Boss Man back.

When you buy it, you get the live game link plus the game as a single HTML file you can keep and run yourself. One payment of $2.99. Yours forever.

How it plays

  • 7 neighborhoods — each leans prices its own way, and every trip costs you a day
  • 13 goods — priced from real 2026 price research in realistic street units; prices roll daily and street events can spike a price ×4 or crash it
  • The debt clock — the loan shark's $5,500 grows 10% a day; the bank pays 5% a day on what you stash
  • Heat and the law — the more you move, the more wanted stars you earn and the harder the cop tiers come; a bust costs you
  • Street events — jackpots, muggings, and finds that can make or break a run
  • Boss encounters — when the Boss Man shows up: pay, talk, or RUN — an odds-based foot chase through the alleys where your heat sets the odds
  • The look — original graffiti-style art panels on deals and big moments, synthesized sound effects, and a best score saved on your device to chase next run
  • Daily Run (new) — one seeded run per calendar day: the same prices and events for everyone who plays that day, your best score for the day kept on your device, and a plain one-line result you can copy and share. No leaderboard — scores live on each player's own phone, and the game says so

The honest print — here and in the game

  • This is a browser game: it plays from a link or from the downloaded HTML file in a phone or desktop browser. It is not in any app store and there is nothing to install.
  • Your best score and run state live in your browser's local storage on that device only — clear the browser data and they're gone.
  • It works offline once it has loaded; it makes no external requests and asks for no permissions.
  • Hustle City is a fictional game about an illegal street economy — it's a game, not advice, and no real person or place is depicted in it.
