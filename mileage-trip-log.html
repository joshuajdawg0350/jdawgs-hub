What it does

  • Seconds-fast manual trip entry: date prefilled today, odometer start/end (miles computed and shown) or a direct miles figure, purpose in your own words, your own vehicle list, and a category you pick: business, medical, charitable, or personal.
  • Totals always free: miles by week, month, and year, by category, and by vehicle — arithmetic on your entries only.
  • The rate for the trip’s own date: the value line multiplies each trip by the IRS standard rate in force when it was driven. For 2026: business 72.5¢ (Jan 1–Jun 30) then 76¢ (Jul 1–Dec 31); medical 20.5¢ then 23.5¢; charitable 14¢ all year. Every rate row prints its IRS source. Rates are editable for an employer or future-year rate and labeled user-entered.
  • Personal trips done honestly: they count in your miles totals and value at $0 in the value line — never silently re-categorized.
  • Export always free: a full CSV of every trip and every field, plus a printable year summary with the rate table printed on it. Never gated.
  • Labeled SAMPLE driver: two vehicles and a month of trips spanning the Jun 30 / Jul 1 rate split, stored separately and removable without touching your real entries.

Honest limits — read these first

  • Entry is manual: a web app cannot auto-detect drives or track GPS in the background, and this one never claims to. You type the odometer — that is the honest cost of a file you own.
  • A record keeper only: it never determines whether a trip is deductible, never calculates tax owed, and is not tax advice. Whether a trip counts — commuting is the classic exclusion — is a question for the IRS rules or a tax preparer. The value line is labeled exactly what it is: value at the IRS standard rate — arithmetic, not a deduction determination.
  • On this device only: no account, nothing uploaded. Export is the backup.
  • Free options exist, honestly: TripLog moved to free unlimited automatic tracking in 2026 — it is an account-based automatic tracker. MileIQ’s free tier covers 40 drives a month. This log is $4.99 once for the owned downloadable single-file version — no account, uncapped, split-correct rate arithmetic — plus Unlimited Pass inclusion.
  • Disclosed, not verified on your phone yet: the downloaded CSV file’s bytes could not be inspected in the audit sandbox; its content is generated row-for-row from the same trip records the app shows on screen.
