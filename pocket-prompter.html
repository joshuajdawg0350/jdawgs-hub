J-Dawgs Pocket Prompter™

Most smart glasses sold today have no screen — so a scrolling prompter on a phone across the room is the wrong tool. The Pocket Prompter works two ways: big type that scrolls on your phone, and a spoken mode that reads your script into your ear, chunk by chunk, through your glasses or earbuds. Your scripts live on your phone. No account, no subscription, no watermark games.

What it does

  • Visual prompter: big-type auto-scroll at a real words-per-minute rate you set, the current line highlighted at a guide line, font-size control, and a mirror flip for beam-splitter rigs. Huge play/pause buttons.
  • Audio prompter — the glasses mode: your phone speaks the script one sentence-chunk at a time through whatever is in your ear (glasses speakers, earbuds, or the phone itself), with a speed control, a voice picker, pause/resume, and giant Next/Previous buttons with a live chunk counter (like "3 of 12") made for a phone in your hand.
  • Script library on your phone: write, paste, or import a .txt script; every script shows its real word count and how long it takes to read at your speed. Rename and delete are built in. Nothing is uploaded — there is no server.
  • Captures organizer: bring in the day's photos and clips from your glasses or phone and they sort themselves by date, with a one-line caption space per item.
  • Try it in 30 seconds: a built-in SAMPLE script, clearly labeled, runs both prompters end-to-end before you load anything of your own.

Honest limits — read these first

  • No glasses integration — and nobody can sell you one from a web page. This app cannot connect to any glasses maker's private system. It works through your phone's normal audio output (glasses, earbuds, speaker) and with files you pick. That is the whole trick, stated plainly.
  • No voice-follow scrolling. Some prompters listen to you and scroll along. This one does not listen at all: scrolling is timed at the speed you set, plus your own Next/Previous taps. We will never imply otherwise.
  • The spoken voice is your phone's built-in speech voice — not an AI clone of you and not a recorded actor. Quality varies by phone; some phones fetch their speech voices with network help (that is the phone's behavior — this app itself sends your script nowhere).
  • Capture dates come from the files themselves. Sharing or re-saving a photo can strip or change that date; the organizer shows what the file carries and labels it as file metadata.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: the spoken output itself could not be heard in our test rig (it has no speech voices installed — the speech engine was called and its real error surfaced plainly on screen, with no hang), so listen on your phone first with the SAMPLE; and the captures file picker uses your phone's standard file dialog, which our rig cannot operate — the view, date labeling, and empty state are verified.
