What it does

What goes in: five answers about your own app — the stores you ship on, your app's category, its features, your age posture, and the markets you sell in.

What comes out:

  • Per-jurisdiction duty matrix with dates: Texas, Utah, Louisiana, and Brazil (live now), plus California AB 1043 and New York (January 1, 2027) — each jurisdiction marked for your profile as applies, deliberate-check, or no duty identified, with the key dates attached.
  • Copy-ready console checklists: the exact Play Console and App Store Connect steps for your profile, plus the in-app integration steps (the Play Age Signals request pattern and Apple's Declared Age Range path), ready to work through line by line.
  • Per-age-band gating decision tables: a decision row for every age band — 0–12, 13–15, 16–17, 18+, and no-signal — with posture-keyed defaults you can edit and date, so "no change for this band" is a recorded decision instead of a shrug.

  • Exportable dated evidence report: your matrix, decisions, and log exported as a dated report (JSON download, print/PDF view, and copyable text) — the show-your-work file every source on these laws says developers will need.

Runs fully in your browser: nothing you enter is sent anywhere. No account, no upload, no subscription.

Say you ship an 18+ game on Google Play only, with in-app purchases, sold in Texas, California, and Brazil. You answer: Play only / game / IAP / 18+ posture / TX + CA + BR.
