J-Dawgs Subscription Reality Check™

Everybody guesses their subscriptions cost less than they do. The tracker apps' answer is another subscription — $7–$15 a month — plus your bank login. Reality Check is $4.99 once, never asks for a bank login, and shows the number that matters: what your list costs per year. What it does

  • True totals, big: enter each service (name, amount, weekly / monthly / yearly / every-N-days cycle, next renewal date, category) and see your true monthly AND true yearly cost computed from your list.
  • Per-service yearly equivalents: a $7.99-a-week line reads as what it is — about $415 a year.
  • Renewal order: the list sorts by next renewal, and anything renewing within 7 days is flagged plainly.
  • Calendar reminders: export an .ics file with one event per renewal, each carrying 7-day and 1-day alarms.
  • Cancelled ledger: mark a service cancelled and it moves to a ledger showing the dollars-per-year back — arithmetic on your own entries, labeled as such.
  • Try it in 30 seconds: a built-in SAMPLE list, clearly labeled, with its totals computed live by the same math.

Honest limits — read these first

  • It cannot see your bank or card charges. There is no bank connection in this app at all. It only knows what you type in — a subscription you forget to add is not in the total.
  • Totals are arithmetic on your entries — not financial advice and not a bill prediction. Prices change and free trials convert; the app cannot know that.
  • Reminders are calendar files you import — the app sends no notifications itself and cannot push anything.
  • Nothing is uploaded. No account, no bank login, by design; your list lives only on your device.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: the downloaded .ics file landing in a real device's Downloads folder (our test browser cannot persist downloads; the file's contents were verified at the generator and its dates were verified in the app).
