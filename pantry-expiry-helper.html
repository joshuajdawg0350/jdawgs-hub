What it does

  • Fast manual entry: name, place (fridge / freezer / pantry), date type, expiry date, quantity — with your last place remembered and quick date options, so adding an item takes seconds.
  • Use-first home screen: everything sorted by expiry, soonest first, overdue flagged in plain words — open it before you cook or shop.
  • Use-by vs best-by, explained plainly: best-by is a quality date; use-by is the manufacturer's last recommended use date. The app shows which one you recorded.
  • Reminders that are honest: a .ics calendar file with alarms 3 days before and on the day. No fake push notifications.
  • Used-up / wasted ledger: mark items used or wasted and see simple counts from your own entries — no invented dollar savings.
  • Export always free: the full list as CSV plus a printable use-first sheet — never gated, no item cap, ever.
  • Try it first: a labeled SAMPLE pantry runs the whole flow and removes cleanly without touching your real list.

Honest limits — read these first

  • Entry is manual. This app does not scan barcodes or read dates off photos, and never claims to.
  • It never declares food safe or unsafe. It shows the date and label type you recorded; safety and storage decisions stay with you and official food-safety guidance.
  • Reminders are calendar files (.ics), not a notification service.
  • Records are only what you type. Nothing is uploaded; there is no account.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: the actual CSV/.ics downloads landing on a real phone (the .ics contents are verified by the app's built-in self-checks; both exports share one download path).
