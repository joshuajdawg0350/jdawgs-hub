How to play

The Rules of the Street

You've got 30 days, $2,000 in your pocket, and a $5,500 debt to the loan shark. Buy product cheap in one neighborhood, move it across the city, sell it high somewhere else. Every deal plays out right on the corner — watch for the scene panels. When day 30 ends, your score is your net worth: cash + bank + stash value − debt.

The Market

  • Prices are rolled fresh every day in every neighborhood, and each neighborhood only stocks some goods each day. "Not sold here" means no buying — but you can always sell what you carry, anywhere.
  • Watch the ▲▼ arrows: they compare today's price to the last price you saw for that good.
  • Your coat holds 100 slots. Most goods take 1 slot per unit. A pint of Lean takes 4 slots.
  • Floods crash a price to a quarter. Shortages spike it times four. The headlines tell you which is which.

Time & Travel

  • Moving to another neighborhood takes the rest of the day. Pick a spot on the tagged-up city map. When you arrive: interest hits, prices re-roll, and the day counter moves.
  • Wait a Day stays put, moves the day, and cools your heat by 1 star.
  • Debt grows 10% per day, compounding, until it's paid. Pay the shark by phone from anywhere — use the PAY DEBT buttons.
  • The Bank: stash cash in the bank with the DEPOSIT buttons and pull it back out with WITHDRAW. Banked cash earns 5%/day and can't be mugged — muggings and bust fines only ever touch the cash in your pocket, never your bank balance.

Heat

  • The stars up top are your wanted level, 0 to 5. Big sales days, busts, and running from trouble raise it. Quiet days lower it.
  • Every good carries its own heat. Heavy, hot product in your coat raises your bust odds every single day you hold it — fentanyl most of all. Get busted holding fentanyl and you lose everything: stash and cash.
  • The hotter you run, the harder the named tiers come down: Patrol Officer Reyes on the quiet beats, Detective Voss on the busy ones, and Task Force Commander Kane when you're really loud.

The J-Dawg

  • The Boss Man's ride is a blacked-out boss sedan called the J-Dawg. You'll see it parked outside the Loan Shark HQ in Downtown.
  • Run your heat up, run the clock down, or sit on his money too long, and the J-Dawg rolls up on you. You can pay up, run — a foot chase through the alleys, and getting caught costs like a bust — or face him and take what comes.
  • Pay the debt off in full and the money visits stop. Owe him on day 29 or 30 and he's still out there looking for you.
