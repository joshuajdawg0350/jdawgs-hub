What it is

Return Window Vault is a web app you open on your phone — no app-store download, no account, no upload. Add a purchase in seconds (item, store, date, price), and the app computes its last return day from a built-in policy table, counts it down on the home screen, and exports calendar reminders so the deadline lands in your own calendar. When you buy it, you also get it as a single HTML file you can keep and run yourself. One payment of $4.99 covers every future season — no "new edition" re-charge, ever.

What it does

  • The countdown is the home screen — entries sorted by urgency, days left in large numbers, last return day in plain words ("Return by Sat, Jan 31, 2027"), expired items marked plainly, never hidden
  • Deadline math shown on every entry — purchase date + window = return-by date, visible, so you can check it yourself
  • Dated, editable policy table — Apple, Best Buy, Amazon, Walmart, Target, Kohl's, and Macy's standard windows plus the posted 2026 holiday extensions (most run to Jan 31, 2027; Apple products bought on Amazon close Jan 15, 2027), each rule showing its source and "Checked Nov 2026" date — and you can edit any window when a store changes its policy or your membership extends it
  • Gift mode — gifts you received are tracked as their own entry type, counted from the date you enter
  • Calendar reminders, honestly labeled — one .ics export with an alarm 7 days before and 1 day before each deadline, in your own calendar. A web app can't guarantee push notifications, so we don't promise them — calendar alarms are what actually fire
  • Return-day helper + refund ledger — tap an item for what to bring and the store's recorded fee note; mark it returned and the refund lands in a money-recovered ledger
  • Private by design — receipt photos and entries never leave your device; no account, no cloud, works fully offline after it loads

The honest limits — printed here and in the app

  • A computed deadline is not a guarantee the store will accept the return. Policies change and memberships differ. Every deadline is labeled "based on the policy as recorded — confirm with the store before you drive there."
  • No OCR in this version. Receipt photos are stored with the entry, not read. You type the few fields yourself — it takes seconds, and what you typed is exactly what the countdown uses.
  • The policy table is dated ("Checked Nov 2026") and editable for a reason: we do not claim to watch the stores for you.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: the downloaded .ics file's bytes (the audit browser cannot capture downloads — the generator in the shipped code was inspected and emits both alarms per deadline) and receipt-photo display on a real phone (verified by the same on-device code path, not by driving a phone's file picker).
