What it is

This is a music enhancer that runs in your phone's browser. No download, no install, no account, no subscription. You pick a music file from your phone, it plays through the enhancer, and the sound goes out to whatever your phone is already connected to — your Bluetooth speaker, your headphones, your car.

It makes your music genuinely louder — not by cranking it into distortion, but with a real loudness chain: compression and limiting, the same way radio stations make songs sound big. Then you shape it with a full equalizer and a bass booster, and prove every bit of it works with the A/B bypass: one tap switches between your original sound and the enhanced sound, same song, same moment.

Plain talk about what it can't do: it plays your own music files — the songs on your phone. No browser app can reach inside another app's stream, so it cannot process Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube while those apps play. What plays through here gets the full treatment. Anything that promises more than that isn't telling you the truth.

What it does

  • Genuinely louder — a compression + limiting loudness chain (the big LOUDER control) that raises the real volume without the crackle and clipping of fake "volume boosters," with a live gain-reduction meter and an honest "Pushing hard" warning when you drive it past clean
  • 10-band graphic equalizer — 31, 62, 125, 250, 500, 1k, 2k, 4k, 8k, and 16k Hz, ±12 dB each, so you can fix dull, muddy, or harsh sound yourself
  • 3 parametric EQ bands — the complicated kind, done right: pick the exact frequency, the boost or cut, and the width (Q). This is the tool the pros use to hunt down one bad frequency
  • Bass booster — low-shelf plus clean saturation, with a numeric readout, so the bottom end hits harder without turning to mush
  • Stereo widening — spread the sound out so it feels bigger on headphones and speakers
  • Presets — Bass Blast, Vocal Clear, Treble Bright, Car, Party, Late Night, and Flat — plus save your own presets and keep them
  • A/B bypass proof — tap it or hold it: your untouched original sound vs. the enhanced sound, instantly. If an enhancer won't let you hear the before, ask yourself why
  • Live meters — a real-time spectrum and output level display, so you can see the enhancement working, not just take our word for it
  • Full player built in — play/pause, seek, track time, volume; works the second it opens with a built-in demo track
  • Runs anywhere — in the browser on your phone or computer, and as a single downloadable HTML file that's yours to keep

Every control on it was probe-tested before it shipped: the number on the screen is what the sound engine is actually doing. If it says +6 dB at 62 Hz, the meter shows +6 dB at 62 Hz.
