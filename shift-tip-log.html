What it does

  • Seconds-fast shift entry: job (your own list, unlimited), date prefilled to today, hours, cash tips, card tips, tip-out, optional base wage and sales.
  • True hourly, shown as arithmetic: (wage × hours + cash + card − tip-out) ÷ hours, printed where the number appears so you can check it. No wage entered? It says “tips per hour” and labels it exactly that — never a blended rate built on a missing wage. A tip-out bigger than your tips shows the negative honestly.
  • Totals always free: week, month, and per-job totals (tips, hours, true hourly) shown in full — no stats tier, no history horizon.
  • Export always free: a full CSV of every shift plus a printable year summary to hand a tax preparer. Export is never gated, capped, or sold separately.
  • Labeled SAMPLE worker: two jobs and a week of shifts to run the whole flow first; samples remove cleanly without touching your real entries.

Honest limits — read these first

  • A record keeper only — not tax advice. It never calculates tax owed, never decides whether any tip or worker qualifies for a deduction, and shows no take-home estimate. General information only: under the OBBBA “No Tax on Tips” provision, up to $25,000 of qualified reported tips may be federally deductible for 2025–2028 — whether it applies to you is a question for a tax preparer; this log is the record you can hand them. Gig Tax Resolver explains the forms to expect; this app is your shift-by-shift earnings record.
  • Your entries only: tips you forget to log are not in the record, and totals are arithmetic on entered shifts only — the totals screen says so.
  • On this device only: no account, no sync, nothing uploaded, and not connected to any employer, payroll, or point-of-sale system. A cleared browser can lose a log that was never exported — export is the backup.
  • Free options exist, honestly: Tiploom logs unlimited shifts free with CSV export but saves one job preset (multiple jobs are Pro, iOS), TipKeepr keeps all history free for one active job, and TipsCoach keeps multiple jobs and full history free with exports in Pro. This log is $4.99 once for unlimited jobs + full history + CSV + printable summary in an owned offline file that works on any phone or browser.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: the CSV download ran and reported its shift count, but the downloaded file’s bytes could not be captured inside the audit sandbox — field completeness was verified from the shipped export code (every on-screen field is a CSV column) rather than from the file itself.
