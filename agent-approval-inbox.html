What it is

Agent Approval Inbox is a web app you open on your phone — no app-store download, no account, nothing uploaded. Agents are starting to buy, book, post, and pay on people's behalf, and the bottleneck is permission. Platform approval prompts, where they exist, live inside one vendor's agent. This is the cross-agent inbox: any agent's request — pasted in, imported as a file, or typed in by hand — waits in one place for your decision, with a record of what you approved, when, and on what terms.

When you buy it, you also get it as a single HTML file you can keep and run yourself. One payment of $6.99. Yours forever.

What it does

  • Plain-language decision cards — "Your agent wants to spend $46 at this store on these items": agent, action, amount, and counterparty up front; the exact payload one tap deeper; expiry shown
  • Approve / Deny / Approve with edits — cap the amount, change the counterparty, attach a note; the decision recorded is the one you actually made
  • Hash-chained approval log — every decision is chained to the previous entry's SHA-256 hash, so edits show; a Verify screen recomputes the chain and tells you plainly whether it holds
  • Export / import in open formats — JSON and CSV, for taxes, disputes, or an employer; importing a log whose chain fails is refused before it touches yours
  • Standing rules that flag, never approve — flag anything over an amount you set, or any new counterparty. Flagged requests wait for you like everything else. Nothing is ever auto-approved
  • Demo mode — four labeled SAMPLE requests (a purchase, a booking, a post, a payment) so you can feel the whole flow in 30 seconds; samples never enter your real log unless you decide one, and then they're marked demo-sourced
  • A documented format for your agent — the request schema printed in-app in copy-paste form, so any agent framework can format requests this inbox understands
  • Private by design — everything is stored on your device; works fully offline after it loads

The honest limits — printed here and in the app

  • V1 is local-first. Requests arrive by paste, file, or manual entry — no remote agent is connected to this inbox, and it does not pretend one is.
  • The inbox reviews and records; it cannot reach into another app and stop an agent. An approval here is evidence of your consent, not a technical block on the agent.
  • The hash chain makes edits evident, not impossible — it is a tamper-evidence record kept on your own device, not a notarized ledger.
  • A live pairing endpoint (agents pushing requests directly) is a possible future version — it is not a claim about this one.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: the two file-picker inputs (request-file import and log-file import — the audit sandbox has no file-upload action; both route through the same validator and chain check proven live via paste and in test harnesses), and download-to-disk landing (the in-page export text box is the verified fallback).
