What it does

  • Unlimited habits: no cap, no counter, no upgrade screen — daily, specific-weekday, or N-times-per-week schedules in plain words.
  • One-tap done + honest streaks: current streak, best streak, and completion rate computed from your recorded taps, with the counting rule printed right under the numbers (what counts as a scheduled day, how a miss breaks a streak, how N-per-week is counted).
  • History calendar per habit: done / missed-scheduled / not-scheduled visually distinct; tap any past day to correct it and streaks recompute.
  • Export always free: a full CSV of every habit and every recorded day. Never gated — the anti-Strides promise.
  • Reminders as .ics calendar files only: add them to your own calendar. No push-notification claim, ever.
  • Labeled SAMPLE habits: 3 habits with a month of history to run the whole flow first, isolated from your real habits.
