Who this is for

You're about to pay a deposit, buy equipment, or hire a company you've never visited — and something feels unverifiable from a screen. A local Hand walks in during business hours and photographs the storefront, the signage, the posted hours, and whether the lights are on and the doors are open. You get dated photos and a short note on what they found — proof the business exists and is operating, before your money moves.

How it works

  1. Post the visit and set your price. Name the business and its area, and what to photograph. The full posted price is the worker's pay.
  2. A local Hand claims it and makes the visit, taking the exact photos you specified.
  3. You approve the photo proof, then payment releases. You pay your posted price plus a 15% platform fee — post a $40 visit and the worker gets $40, the fee is $6, you pay $46. Nothing is due before the proof is in and approved.
