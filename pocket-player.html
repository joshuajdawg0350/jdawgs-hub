What it is — a player, plain and simple

Pocket Player is a music player. You open your own audio files in it — the songs already on your phone or computer — and it plays them with a real sound chain built in: EQ, bass, and loudness, plus per-song leveling so a quiet track and a loud track meet in the middle.

To be plain about what it is not: this is not a music studio. It does not make, record, or mix music — if you want to create songs, that's our separate J-Dawgs Universal Music Maker. Pocket Player is for listening to the music you already have, and hearing it louder and clearer.

It runs in your browser on any phone — no app-store download, no account — and you can add it to your home screen like an app. When you buy it, you also get it as a single HTML file you can keep and run yourself. One payment of $9.99. Yours forever.

What it does

  • 10-band EQ + bass boost — shape the sound the way you like it, with presets to start from
  • Louder control — a compressor + limiter chain that brings quiet files up to full, clear volume without the distortion "booster" apps sell as loudness
  • A/B bypass with a live level meter — one switch flips between the plain original and the enhanced sound, so you hear the difference yourself instead of trusting a claim. It's a manual switch: tap to hear the plain original, it stays there until you tap back
  • Per-song leveling — the player scans your songs and evens out the loud ones and the quiet ones, so you're not riding the volume button track to track
  • Sleep timer — set it and the music stops on its own
  • Private by design — your files are played on your device and never leave it. Works fully offline once loaded

The honest limits — printed here and in the app

  • It plays files opened in it only. It cannot enhance Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, TikTok, or any other streaming app's audio — no web app can, and any that claims otherwise isn't telling the truth.
  • It plays your music; it does not record, make, or edit songs. Creation is the Universal Music Maker's job, not this app's.
  • Honesty about our own testing: the sound chain (EQ, bass, Louder, bypass, leveling) was verified in the build audit, but real-file playback behavior, the measurable audio change on a real phone's speaker or headphones, and the sleep timer firing over a real listening session were not exercised with a real audio file in our test sandbox — those are real-device checks, and we'd rather say so than dress them up. If it doesn't perform on your device, the Fix-It Guarantee below is the remedy, and it has no fine print.
  • Sound still comes out of your phone's own speaker or your headphones — a player can't add hardware you don't have.
