What it does

  • Printable family plan card: household members, out-of-town contact, two meeting places (near home + outside the neighborhood), utility shut-off locations you type, and a pets note. Print it for the fridge — the printed card is how the plan is shared.
  • Go-bag checklist per person: water, food, first aid, light, radio, documents, cash, medicines, clothes — from public guidance (FEMA / Ready.gov / American Red Cross, named in the app, restated in our own words). Quantities are guidance arithmetic from your household size: water is 1 gallon per person per day × 3 days, so 4 people = 12 gallons, computed live.
  • Supply-expiry rotation: dated supplies sorted soonest-first with plain-word overdue / expiring-soon flags, and .ics calendar reminders at 30, 7, and 1 days before expiry — the recurring job that keeps a kit from quietly going stale.
  • Exports always free: printable plan card + checklist and a CSV of your supplies and plan — never gated, ever.
  • Labeled SAMPLE household: run the whole flow first; samples remove cleanly without touching your real entries.

Honest limits — read these first

  • Guidance, not official advice, and NOT a warning system — this app never provides live alerts. In a real emergency, follow emergency services. The checklist restates public guidance from FEMA / Ready.gov / the Red Cross in our own words.
  • Quantities are guidance arithmetic on the household size you typed; shut-off locations and contacts are what you typed — the app never claims to know them.
  • Reminders are calendar files (.ics), not push notifications — import them and your own calendar reminds you.
  • Free options exist, honestly: PrepTracker is completely free (no account, offline) with plans and go-bags; GoBagPlus has a genuinely useful free tier (1 bag with expiry reminders — its PDF exports and family sharing are Premium, $4.99/mo / $34.99/yr, account-based); Family Ready's free tier covers the card, plan, checklist, and a Snapshot PDF (reminders and sync are Plus, $9.99/mo / $59.99/yr).   • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: the downloaded .ics file's bytes could not be inspected in the audit session (the download fired without error; its reminders are generated from the same expiry dates shown on screen), and print rendering from a regular browser (the print layout prints only the card and checklist).
