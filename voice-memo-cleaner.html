What it is

Voice Memo Cleaner is a web app you open on your phone — no app-store download, no account. Record a memo in it, or open any memo, interview, or vocal take you already have. It shows the waveform with dead air highlighted against a threshold it prints on screen, then cleans the recording in one tap and lets you hear the original against the cleaned version at the same passage before you save anything.

When you buy it, you also get it as a single HTML file you can keep and run yourself. One payment of $6.99. Yours forever.

What it does

  • Record or open — record in-app with the microphone, or open any audio file; a built-in test recording lets you try the whole flow first
  • Waveform with real silence highlighting — dead air is found from actual sample levels against a shown threshold, not guessed
  • One-tap Clean & Level — trims the counted silence, evens loudness with compression/limiting, applies a gentle speech-clarity EQ, and runs a measured ceiling pass so peaks stay at or under −1 dBFS
  • Measured before/after table — peak, average level, and exactly how many seconds of dead air were removed, counted from your recording
  • A/B preview + Undo — flip between Original and Cleaned mid-playback; undo restores the original, with confirmation
  • Honest filters — a rumble/wind high-pass and a hum notch, labeled as filtering only. They are never sold as AI noise removal
  • Genuine exports — real 24-bit WAV and real 256 kbps MP3, named with title + date, plus an optional notes file. No transcription is offered or claimed
  • Private by design — everything is processed on your device; files never leave your phone; works offline after it loads

The honest limits — printed here and in the app

  • Microphone quality depends on your phone's mic and OS permissions. A recording made next to a running saw cannot be made clean by any honest filter — this app cleans what is cleanable and says so.
  • The filters are filters. They reduce rumble and hum; they do not separate voices from noise and are not AI enhancement.
  • There is no transcription. The notes field is for your own words.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build sandbox: the OS file picker itself (it shares the verified decode path), a successful mic capture (the audit browser has no mic — the denied-permission path is verified), and dragging the threshold slider (its recompute was verified at the default −45 dB through the same analysis code). Those are real-device checks, stated plainly.
