What it does

  • Manual entry, plain and fast: title + author typed by you, optional page count and notes. No account, nothing uploaded.
  • Three-status shelf: Want to read / Reading / Finished, with a finish date recorded when a book is finished (you can edit it).
  • Session notes: dated pages-read notes per book, in your own words.
  • Statistics — ALWAYS free: totals by status, total pages (with the counting rule printed on screen), books finished this year, and a plain year-in-review. No statistic is ever gated, capped, or teased as premium — there is no premium tier.
  • CSV export — always free: every field, including per-session rows. Export is the backup, and it is never paywalled.
  • Labeled SAMPLE shelf: sample books run the whole flow and remove cleanly without touching your real log.

Honest limits — read these first

  • A private log, not a discovery service — no recommendations, no social layer, no book database, no cover art fetched from anywhere, no ISBN or barcode scanning. Entry is manual, stated plainly.
  • No reading timer — sessions are your own dated notes, not a clock claim. Statistics are arithmetic on the entries you typed; unentered page counts and dates are not counted, and the stats screen says so.
  • Free options exist, honestly: Goodreads is completely free (an account-based social platform run by Amazon), Hardcover’s free tier covers tracking (account-based cloud), and Endleaf is free on iPhone. This log is $2.99 once for a different thing: an owned offline file that works on any phone or browser, with stats and export never gated and no account at all.
  • Disclosed-not-verified in our build audit: the single-session delete button and the storage-blocked fallback notice share code paths with probe-verified handlers (every storage call is wrapped so a restricted browser falls back to session-only instead of breaking). Records live on this device only; export a CSV as your backup.
