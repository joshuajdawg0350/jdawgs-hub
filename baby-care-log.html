What it does

  • Big-button logging, one hand: Feed (side and amount exactly as you type them), Sleep start / stop, and Diaper (wet / dirty / both) — buttons sized for one thumb at 3 a.m.
  • Last fed / last slept / last changed: the three answers a caregiver actually needs, on the home screen, computed from the entries on this device.
  • Totals are arithmetic only: counts of feeds and diapers and sleep time summed from your entries, today and all days. Sums, never judgments.
  • Printable 24-hour handover page: the last 24 hours on one printed page to hand to a partner, grandparent, or sitter.
  • Export always free: a full CSV of every entry. Never gated.
  • Labeled SAMPLE baby: a sample day to run the whole flow first, stored separately and removable without touching your real entries.

Safety and honest limits — read these first

  • A log only: it never gives sleep predictions, never gives feeding or sleep advice, never makes growth or medical judgments, and never shows percentile or “normal range” claims. If your baby seems unwell, contact a clinician — do not consult a log.
  • Single device: this app cannot live-sync two phones. The printed handover page is the sharing method, and it is labeled as a page, never as sync.
  • On this device only: no account, nothing uploaded. Export is the backup.
  • Free options exist, honestly: Robin Baby has a genuinely free plan (unlimited logging, CSV export, sharing with up to 3 caregivers) — it is account- and cloud-based, its free insights cover 14 days, and its doctor-ready PDF is a Premium feature.
