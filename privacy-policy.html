The short version

J-Dawgs Bubble Enhancer collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no server. The app does not even request internet access. Everything it does — applying audio effects and remembering your settings — happens on your own device.

No fancy logos. Just quality products. No ads.

What the app does on your device

Bubble Enhancer uses Android's built-in system audio effects (equalizer, bass boost, virtualizer, and loudness processing with a limiter) and applies them to the audio playing on your phone. It shows a small floating bubble so you can turn the effects on or off and adjust them while other apps play music or video. To do that job, it uses these Android permissions, and only for these purposes:

  • Modify audio settings — to create and control the system audio effects. This is the app's core function.
  • Draw over other apps — for the floating bubble. You grant this yourself in Android Settings; the app cannot switch it on by itself.
  • Foreground service — keeps the bubble and the effects running while you listen, with a visible notification so you always know it is on.
  • Notifications — for that "enhancer is running" notice (Android 13+).
  • Battery-optimization exemption — the app may ask you to exempt it so your phone maker does not kill the bubble mid-song. You decide; the app works either way, it may just be stopped by aggressive battery savers.

The app does not record audio. It has no microphone permission and contains no recording code — it adjusts the sound your phone is already playing; it never listens to anything. A test-tone button plays a short generated tone through your speaker so you can hear the effect change; that tone is generated on the device and nothing is captured.

What the app stores, and where

Your effect settings — the preset you picked, equalizer band levels, bass and loudness levels, and whether the enhancer is on — are stored in the app's own private storage on your device, so they are still there when you come back. That information never leaves your device: the app requests no network permission (you can confirm there is no Internet permission in the app listing and in Android's app info screen), and it contains no code that transmits anything anywhere.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores lives on your device. Clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app, and it is gone. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.
